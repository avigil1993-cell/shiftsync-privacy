# Privacy Policy for ShiftSync

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026  
**Developer:** Vigilant Studios  
**Contact:** vigilantstudios.apps@gmail.com 

Vigilant Studios built **ShiftSync** (`com.vigilantstudios.shiftsync`) as a freemium utility application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ShiftSync.

## 1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
ShiftSync does not require account registration and does not operate external user-data servers. All shift schedules, rotation patterns, single-day overrides, sleep duration targets, caffeine cutoff preferences, and supplement reminder settings are stored **locally on your device** using Android's local Room database. Vigilant Studios does not collect, transmit, or have access to your personal schedule or sleep preferences.

## 2. Permissions Used
- **Alarms & Reminders (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) and Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly on-device to trigger local caffeine cutoff and wind-down reminders at your configured times.

## 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
ShiftSync integrates the following Google services, which may automatically collect and process certain device and interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:
- **Google AdMob:** Displays banner and optional rewarded video advertisements in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, approximate IP-based location, app interaction events, and crash/performance diagnostics to serve and measure ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising through your Android device's **Settings > Google > Ads** menu.
- **Google Play Billing:** Processes optional one-time in-app purchases (`shiftsync_pro_lifetime`) to remove advertisements and unlock Pro features. Payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play; Vigilant Studios only receives non-financial purchase confirmation tokens.

Links to third-party privacy policies:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob & AdSense Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

## 4. Children's Privacy
ShiftSync is intended for adult shift workers (ages 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 5. Data Deletion
Because all shift and preference data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling ShiftSync.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
Vigilant Studios may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
