# Privacy Policy for AnchorHours

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**Developer:** Vigilant Studios  
**Contact:** vigilantstudios.apps@gmail.com  

Vigilant Studios built **AnchorHours** (`com.vigilantstudios.anchorhours`) as a utility application with an optional Pro upgrade. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use AnchorHours.

## 1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
AnchorHours does not require account registration and does not operate external user-data servers. All shift schedules, rotation patterns, single-day overrides, sleep duration targets, caffeine cutoff preferences, and supplement reminder settings are stored **locally on your device** using Android's local Room database. Vigilant Studios does not collect, transmit, or have access to your personal schedule or sleep preferences.

## 2. Permissions Used
- **Alarms & Reminders (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) and Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly on-device to trigger local caffeine cutoff and wind-down reminders at your configured times.

## 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
AnchorHours integrates the following Google service, which may automatically collect and process certain device and interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:
- **Google Play Billing:** Processes optional one-time in-app purchases (`anchorhours_pro_lifetime`) to unlock Pro features. Payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play; Vigilant Studios only receives non-financial purchase confirmation tokens.

Link to third-party privacy policy:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Children's Privacy
AnchorHours is intended for adult shift workers (ages 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 5. Data Deletion
Because all shift and preference data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling AnchorHours.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
Vigilant Studios may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
